Where the words you couldn't say finally have a place.

Some things are too difficult to say out loud. Some people never know what they meant to us. And some words stay with us long after the moment has passed.

This is a place for those words.

Friendship

To the friend I drifted away from...

I still think about the years when talking to you was the easiest thing in the world. Somewhere between growing up and becoming different people, we stopped calling. I don't think either of us meant for it to happen.

I hope you're doing well. And I hope you know that losing touch never made those years mean less to me.

Kindness

To the classmate who smiled at me when I was having a terrible day...

You probably don't remember it.

You walked past me, smiled, and asked if I was okay. I said yes because I didn't know what else to say. But I remember that small moment.

Sometimes people don't realize how much a tiny act of kindness can mean to someone who is struggling quietly.

School

To the people who made me feel like I didn't belong...

For a long time, I thought there must be something wrong with me. I wondered why being myself seemed to make me an easy target.

I'm slowly learning that someone's decision to be unkind doesn't decide my worth. I wish I had understood that sooner.

Family

To the version of my family that I remember...

I know things change. Adults change. Families change. Everyone keeps telling me that I'll get used to it.

Maybe they're right. But I think it's okay to miss the way things used to be while still learning how to live with the way they are now.

To Myself

To the younger me...

You spent so much time worrying about whether you were good enough.

I wish I could tell you that growing up isn't about becoming perfect. It's about becoming someone who understands themselves a little better.

You were doing better than you thought.
